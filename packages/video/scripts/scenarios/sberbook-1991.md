## Deviations
- The brief's hook said «В девяносто первом это были Жигули»: no source for the 1991 retail price of a ВАЗ was found, so the line became «полтора года зарплаты» (548 руб./мес, Росстат). PLAYBOOK §10: a figure without a source does not go in.
- The base of the price index is December 1991, not 20 June 1991 (Росстат's annual series starts there); the real loss is therefore understated, not overstated.
- Two readout lines in the frame (В ЦЕНАХ 1991 / НА КНИЖКЕ), as in salary-2013 — one big hero number, the second small and dim.
