# Khaby Lame — The Power of Silence

- **id**: khaby-silence
- **format**: reel (1080×1920), ~55s
- **style**: premium editorial mini-documentary. Nike/Apple negative space,
  fashion-magazine typography (oversized grotesk + a serif italic for one word
  per headline), creator-economy doc pacing, restrained data-viz. Black /
  bone-white / neutral greys; one accent, used flat — never as neon glow.
- **hero color**: editorial vermilion `#E8481F`. Appears on at most one element
  per frame, and most frames have none. No second signal color.
- **hook (first 2s)**: 0.4s of black, then **0** slams, then **WORDS.** —
  and the hero portrait rises through the type with letters masked in front of
  and behind his head.
- **language**: English
- **photos**: real, CC-licensed, matted with `scripts/matte.swift` (Vision
  framework), graded to greyscale at render time — see
  `public/images/CREDITS.md`. Never a synthesised likeness.
  - `khaby-hero.png` — Web Summit 2025 portrait (CC BY 4.0): the hook and the
    UNIVERSAL reveal.
  - `khaby-gesture.png` — the open-palms gesture, from State Farm's 2023 shoot
    (CC BY 2.0): the OPPOSITE beat and the globe.
  - `khaby-stage.png` / `khaby-calm.png` — Web Summit 2025 (CC BY 4.0): the #1
    editorial-flash frame and the barrier reveal.

## Beats

1. **hook** (5s) — black → `0` → `WORDS.` → portrait rises through the type
   (letters masked behind/in front) → KHABY LAME / THE POWER OF *SILENCE* →
   follower count climbs 1M · 10M · 50M · 100M+ set into the composition.
2. **job** (6.5s) — `2020` enormous → FACTORY JOB, a rule strikes through,
   LOST. → ladder FACTORY ↓ BEDROOM ↓ TIKTOK → a phone tilts in 3D:
   @khaby.lame · 0 FOLLOWERS. The quiet, intimate beat.
3. **nobody** (5.5s) — illustrative early-post cards slide past with tiny view
   counts (labelled as illustrative, not statistics) → everything fades → 0.3s
   of nothing → THEN HE NOTICED SOMETHING. with a slow push-in.
4. **chaos** (6s) — abstract life-hack diagrams pile up: STEP 1…6, arrows,
   annotations, a banana opened by a machine, five tools for one cut; the
   frame gets busier until it is noise → HARD CUT TO BLACK.
5. **opposite** (5s) — total reset. One object, one line, Khaby's open palms
   rise in → THAT'S IT. Held. The joke is how simple it gets.
6. **silence** (7.5s) — NO EXPLANATION. / NO VOICE. / NO LANGUAGE. slam in
   turn → zoom out to a dark dot-globe, points light up (USA, BRAZIL, ITALY,
   INDIA, JAPAN, NIGERIA, MEXICO, FRANCE) and greetings orbit (HELLO · CIAO ·
   HOLA · BONJOUR · こんにちは · OLÁ) → every word vanishes; only the gesture
   stays → NO TRANSLATION NEEDED.
7. **barrier** (5.5s) — a typographic wall (ENGLISH · SPANISH · FRENCH · ARABIC
   · PORTUGUESE · ITALIAN · JAPANESE) the camera drives into; on "language" it
   shatters into slats and slides away; behind it the portrait → UNIVERSAL.
8. **growth** (6s) — vertical ladder 2020 → 1M → 10M → 50M → 100M+, the camera
   rides up faster and faster; KHABY LAME, then a huge `#1` with editorial
   camera flashes and the stage photo. Screenshot frame.
9. **lesson** (7.5s) — three conceptual creator cards (A 2.4M · B 850K ·
   C 320K); FOLLOWERS is struck and replaced by AUDIENCE FIT; LANGUAGE ·
   COUNTRY · INTERESTS · ENGAGEMENT · AUDIENCE stack in; REACH ≠ RELEVANCE →
   THE RIGHT AUDIENCE WINS.
10. **yoloco** (5.5s) — black. A small Yoloco mark, FIND THE RIGHT CREATORS.,
    yoloco.io, the photo credit line. One second of hold.

Pacing: new visual information every 1–2 seconds, but every cut is attached
to the sentence. The contrasts are the grammar — CHAOS → SIMPLICITY, WORDS →
SILENCE, LOCAL → GLOBAL, FOLLOWERS → AUDIENCE FIT — and the piece should read
with the sound off.

## Voiceover

- `01-hook`: This man became one of the biggest creators on Earth... without
  saying a single word.
- `02-job`: In 2020, Khaby Lame lost his factory job. So he went home... opened
  TikTok... and started filming videos in his bedroom.
- `03-nobody`: At first, almost nobody cared. Then he noticed something.
- `04-chaos`: The internet was obsessed with ridiculous life hacks. People were
  making simple things... impossibly complicated.
- `05-opposite`: So Khaby did the opposite. He showed the obvious solution.
- `06-silence`: No explanation. No voice-over. No language. And suddenly... the
  entire world understood the joke.
- `07-barrier`: Khaby had removed one of the biggest barriers in content:
  language.
- `08-growth`: And the result? He became the most-followed creator on TikTok.
- `09-lesson`: But here's the influencer marketing lesson. The best creator
  isn't always the one who says the most. It's the one your audience
  understands instantly.
- `10-yoloco`: That's why brands don't just need big creators. They need the
  right creators. Yoloco.

Caption pages are hidden (`~`) wherever a headline carries the words: the
hook's SINGLE WORD, the three NO … slams, NO TRANSLATION, LANGUAGE, #1, the
lesson's closing line, and the brand line. Highlighted words: BIGGEST, LOST,
OPPOSITE, WORLD, UNIVERSAL, AUDIENCE, RIGHT.

## Facts

"Most-followed creator on TikTok" is stated in the past tense on purpose —
Khaby took the #1 spot in June 2022 (MrBeast passed him in 2024), so the #1
frame carries a small `2022` tag. The early-post view counts in beat 3 and the
creator cards in beat 9 are labelled illustrative; nothing on screen is
presented as analytics.
