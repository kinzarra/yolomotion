# The Creator War — YouTube vs Netflix

- **id**: youtube-netflix
- **format**: reel (1080×1920, 30fps), ~57s — the brief's 52–58s window, and
  never past 60
- **series**: standalone — editorial register, kit re-exported from
  `khaby-silence` (black page, bone type, one flat red), with the
  documentary/data-viz devices of `mrbeast-100k` (mono folios, archival
  cards, counters) grafted on
- **hero color**: platform red `#E50914` — **and that is the whole idea**.
  YouTube's red and Netflix's red are the same red, so the reel spends exactly
  one red and the two platforms are told apart by *shape* alone (YouTube = the
  rounded play plate, Netflix = the vertical N bars). One red element per
  frame, except in the deliberate versus frames where the collision of the two
  marks IS the event. Creators, money and every number are bone — the money is
  never green, the winner is never gold. No second signal color.
- **hook (first 2s)**: the studio face full-bleed, mid-sentence, `BREAKING ·
  CREATOR ECONOMY` folio; on "millions" the camera pulls back into a broadcast
  plate and `$MILLIONS` slams across the frame *behind* the plate's edge.
- **face**: two inserts — face-led hook 0–4.6s (broadcast plate, the type
  crosses behind it) and a 2.2s interrupt at 0:35. ~6.8s total, inside the
  brief's 6–7s cap.

## Beats

1. **hook** — full-bleed studio. On *millions* the frame pulls back: the video
   becomes a tall plate on the right, `$MILLIONS` slams across the whole width
   behind its edge (the brief's "letters behind the subject", earned by
   composition rather than by matting the studio away). The YouTube plate and
   the Netflix N stack at the left on a hairline. `STAY` / *exclusive.* slams
   lower-left — one serif-italic word, the khaby move. On *exclusive* the two
   marks tear apart left and right and the camera pushes **through** the word
   into beat 02.
2. **offer** — black. Bloomberg strip at the top: `CREATOR ECONOMY · EXCLUSIVITY
   · TALENT`. The YouTube plate draws itself; three abstract cards
   `CREATOR 01/02/03` step in below it; `$1M+` `$2M+` `$5M+` fly in beside them
   in bone. Money flows YouTube ↓ creators as travelling dots. Then the Netflix
   N cuts in from the right and the flow stops dead.
   *On-screen mono footnote, not a caption:* `CONCEPTUAL — REPORTED
   MULTI-MILLION-DOLLAR INCENTIVES. INDIVIDUAL TERMS VARY.`
3. **netflix** — the cinematic beat. Black, then one red hairline draws across
   the frame. The **N** builds out of three bars and goes enormous. Camera
   pulls back: `NETFLIX` right, `YOUTUBE` left, `CREATORS` bone between them on
   the hairline. Then `THE CREATOR WAR` fills the page — no glow, no outline,
   no bevel: it is a masthead, not a thumbnail.
4. **television** — the same abstract show card jumps `PHONE → LAPTOP → TV`,
   each device drawn in hairlines, the card growing at every hop. The TV then
   opens to fill the whole frame and the type lands inside it:
   `CREATORS` / `ARE` / `TELEVISION.` Screenshot frame #1.
5. **problem** — one premium show card centre-frame. It splits in two: left
   under `YOUTUBE`, right under `NETFLIX`, identical. `EXCLUSIVE VALUE 100%`
   degrades to `???` (labelled `CONCEPTUAL`). Everything drops away and the
   frame goes near-silent under `WHY WATCH HERE?`.
6. **money** — the transfer board. `OLD MODEL`: CREATOR ↓ UPLOAD ↓ ADS ↓
   REVENUE SHARE, struck through in red. `NEW BATTLE`: PLATFORM ↓ $$$ ↓
   CREATOR ↓ EXCLUSIVE CONTENT, `+ BRAND DEALS`. A creator card slides toward
   the YouTube side while the Netflix N hauls on it from the right — a
   deadline-day tug, held one frame before it lands.
7. **crazy** — face insert #2. Full bleed, pushed in and framed left, so it is
   nothing like the hook's plate. Behind/right: `CREATORS` re-sets into
   `THE NEW` / `HOLLYWOOD?`, cropped by the frame edge. Cut on the last word.
8. **shift** — the key moment. `CREATORS` `vs` `HOLLYWOOD` in bone; the block
   physically rotates on its own axis and lands as `YOUTUBE` `vs` `NETFLIX` in
   red — then, enormous underneath, `FOR CREATORS.` Camera flashes, contract
   rules, ticker figures. Screenshot frame #2.
9. **followers** — `10,000,000` fills the page over `FOLLOWERS`. `SO WHAT?`
   slams; the number collapses through the floor and reveals what was under
   it: `AUDIENCE` `TRUST` `BRAND FIT` `ENGAGEMENT` `GEOGRAPHY` `INTERESTS`.
   Then `ATTENTION` / `IS AN ASSET.`
10. **economy** — pull back to the ecosystem: YOUTUBE, NETFLIX, BRANDS,
    CREATORS, VIEWERS as nodes over a field of small creator marks, all wired
    in hairlines. Then it simplifies: everything dims, one creator card and one
    brand card remain, a red line draws between them.
    `THE RIGHT CREATOR × THE RIGHT AUDIENCE`.
11. **yoloco** — black. Mark, `Yoloco`, `FIND THE CREATORS / WORTH BETTING ON.`,
    `yoloco.io`. One-second hold.

No `улика:` lines: every beat is graphic. The brief names Alan Chikin Chow and
Nick DiGiovanni as *optional* creators and forbids fabricated photographs —
no licensed editorial photography of them was supplied to this project, so
the reel uses abstract creator cards throughout and shows no likeness at all.

## Captions

Shared `<Captions>`, white active word, red for `+WORD`. Most pages are
`~hidden`: the brief asks for spoken language to become part of the motion
design rather than a subtitle strip, so wherever a headline is already saying
those words (`$MILLIONS`, `STAY EXCLUSIVE`, `THE CREATOR WAR`, `CREATORS ARE
TELEVISION`, `WHY WATCH HERE?`, `THE NEW HOLLYWOOD?`, `FOR CREATORS`,
`SO WHAT?`, the Yoloco line) the caption stays out and the frame never carries
the same sentence twice. Exactly three words are painted red across the whole
reel: `+MILLIONS` (02), `+PURSUING` (04) and `+ATTENTION` (09).

## Deviations

- **The avatar is generated, not stubbed.** The brief asks for
  `<HeyGenAvatarHook />` placeholders fed by externally supplied footage. The
  user instead set a HeyGen budget for this reel, so both inserts are really
  generated and lip-synced to the house ElevenLabs clips — which is why
  `durations.ts` and every caption timing stay derived from the same mp3s.
- **Voiceover trimmed against the brief's full read.** The brief's script
  measures ~59s spoken; the same script must fit inside 58s *with* holds. Every
  block and every ТЕКСТ НА ЭКРАНЕ is kept; supporting clauses are cut
  ("According to reports, YouTube is discussing multi-million-dollar deals with
  **some of** its biggest creators" → without "some of"; "what **exactly** makes
  YouTube exclusive" → without "exactly"). No claim changed.
- **Music and sound design are carried visually.** This repo's reels are
  voiceover-only (standing decision). The brief's bass impacts become frame
  shakes and one-frame white flashes; the "drop the music before *and this is
  where it gets crazy*" becomes a hard black frame and a cut; the near-silence
  under `WHY WATCH HERE?` is a beat where nothing moves but the vignette.
- **Two reds resolved into one.** Using YouTube `#FF0000` next to Netflix
  `#E50914` reads as a colour-management fault, not as a duel. The reel uses one
  red for both and separates the platforms by form. It also happens to be the
  story's point.
- **No creator photography, no logos-as-supplied-assets.** The platform marks
  are drawn as geometry in the reel's own red (a play plate, three N bars) —
  recognisable, never a pasted brand asset, never implying endorsement.
- **Every number is labelled.** `$1M+ / $2M+ / $5M+` carry `CONCEPTUAL` type in
  the same frame, `EXCLUSIVE VALUE 100% → ???` carries it too, and the spoken
  line says "according to reports … multi-million-dollar deals", never a
  per-creator figure. Nothing in the reel says a deal is signed.
