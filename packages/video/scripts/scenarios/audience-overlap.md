# Five Influencers. Five Invoices. One Audience.

- **id**: audience-overlap
- **format**: reel (1080×1920), ~30s
- **hero color**: electric green `#00FF85` (Yoloco, the fix, new reach);
  red `#FF3B4D` as the equally scarce problem signal
- **hook (first 2s)**: five payment cards fan out, one per influencer —
  five payments leaving for what turns out to be one audience
- **loop**: the finale returns to the opening cards, so the Short replays clean

## Beats

1. **hook** (6s) — 5 cards + payments → 5 INFLUENCERS → 1 AUDIENCE
2. **overlap** (4.5s) — follower clouds converge, duplicates flip red
3. **counter** (5s) — TOTAL FOLLOWERS races up, then UNIQUE REACH deflates
4. **invoices** (4.5s) — invoice stack, INVISIBLE INFLUENCER TAX stamps across it
5. **yoloco** (6s) — overlap analysis, swap creators, reach expands
6. **finale** (4s) — STOP BUYING FOLLOWERS → START BUYING UNIQUE REACH → logo

Pacing: rapid cuts through the hook, a deliberate slow-down on the
invisible-tax reveal, then acceleration again for the fix.

## Voiceover

- `01-hook`: You just paid five different influencers. Congratulations — you
  may have bought the same audience five times.
- `02-overlap`: Because different creators can share thousands of identical
  followers.
- `03-reach`: Follower counts make your campaign look massive. Unique reach
  tells the real story.
- `04-tax`: This is the invisible influencer tax — and most brands never see it.
- `05-yoloco`: Yoloco reveals audience overlap before you spend — so every
  creator adds new reach, not another invoice.
- `06-question`: Would you rather hire five influencers — or reach five
  different audiences?

Highlighted words: `IDENTICAL` red; `YOLOCO`, `NEW REACH` green. The pages
covering "THE SAME AUDIENCE / FIVE TIMES", "UNIQUE REACH" and "INVISIBLE
INFLUENCER TAX" are hidden (`~`) — the scene headlines carry those words.
