# The Influencer Who Doesn't Exist

- **id**: ai-influencer
- **format**: reel (1080×1920), ~30s
- **hero color**: red `#FF2E3F` on black/white — everything else monochrome,
  and she is rendered in porcelain neutrals so she reads synthetic
- **hook (first 2s)**: slow push-in on a face that looks like a real
  influencer, then a glitch — SHE DOESN'T EXIST
- **loop**: the finale glitches back into the opening shot

## Beats

1. **hook** (4.5s) — push-in → glitch → SHE DOESN'T EXIST
2. **morph** (5.5s) — niche morphs, 24/7 / FULL CONTROL / UNLIMITED
3. **split** (5.5s) — tired human creator vs the AI multiplying itself
4. **trust** (5.5s) — freeze, pixel breakup → WOULD YOU TRUST HER?
5. **scale** (4.5s) — AI SCALE vs HUMAN AUTHENTICITY
6. **finale** (4.5s) — eyes close-up → YES / NO → loop glitch

Pacing: fast cuts up to the trust beat, then a deliberate slow-down — the
freeze and pixel breakup are the contrast the piece is built around.

## Voiceover

- `01-hook`: This influencer promotes real products. But there's one problem...
  she doesn't exist.
- `02-ai`: She's an AI influencer. Perfect appearance. Unlimited content. No
  days off. And no scandals.
- `03-brands`: For brands, she sounds perfect. She's never late, never tired,
  and she can create hundreds of videos.
- `04-trust`: But there's one thing AI still struggles to generate
  convincingly... trust.
- `05-believe`: The winner won't be human, or AI. It will be whoever the
  audience believes.
- `06-question`: Would you buy something recommended by a person who never
  existed?

The pages for "SHE DOESN'T EXIST" and "TRUST" are hidden (`~`) — the scene
headlines land those two words instead of the captions.
